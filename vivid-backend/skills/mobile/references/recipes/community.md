# Recipe: community (a club, church, alumni group, creators' circle or neighbourhood app)

Tabs: Feed, Events, Groups (or Members), Profile. Stack: post detail with comments,
event detail, group detail, member profile. Sheets: new post, RSVP options.

Feed: posts as cards (avatar, name, time ago, text with a "more" expander, up to four
photos in a grid, like and comment counts); like with an optimistic heart animation and a
light haptic; pull to refresh; a floating compose button (bottom right, above the tab bar)
opening the new post sheet with text and photos from the image picker.

Post detail: the post, comments as a list, a pinned comment field that stays above the
keyboard.

Events: upcoming as cards with date block, title, place and attendee avatars; detail
with cover image, description, map link, RSVP (Going, Maybe) and Add to calendar.

Groups or Members: searchable directory with roles (Admin, Member), a member profile
with bio and a WhatsApp or message action.

Moderation: admins can pin, hide and delete posts; members can report a post with a
reason.

Data: profiles (name, avatar, bio, role), posts (author, text, photos, pinned, hidden),
comments, likes, events (title, cover, starts, place, description), rsvps, groups.

Minimums for a first build: 20 seeded posts from 10 members with generated portraits
(Black Nigerian people), comments on half of them, 6 upcoming events with covers,
working like, comment, post with photos and RSVP; moderation for admins.

Signature: Posts cascade in; the like is a heart pop with a haptic; a floating glass compose button; swipe cards when the spec is about discovering people or things.
