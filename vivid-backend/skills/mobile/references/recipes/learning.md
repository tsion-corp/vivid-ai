# Recipe: learning (courses, lessons and quizzes for a school, academy, tutor or exam prep)

Tabs: Home, Courses, My learning, Profile. Stack: course detail, lesson player, quiz,
results. Sheets: filters, lesson notes.

Home: continue learning card (course, lesson, progress bar, Resume), today's goal
(minutes or lessons, a ring), recommended courses rail, categories, a streak.

Course detail: cover, title, instructor with avatar, rating, number of lessons and hours,
what you will learn (checklist), the curriculum as sections of lessons (with duration,
lock for paid, check when done), price or Enrol, reviews.

Lesson: video (expo-video) or reading content with headings and images, notes, Mark as
done with a success haptic and the next lesson button; progress saved per lesson.

Quiz: one question per screen with options as large tappable cards, immediate feedback
(correct turns green with a check, wrong shows the right answer and a one-line why), a
progress bar, results with score, time and review of mistakes, Retry.

My learning: enrolled courses with progress, certificates for completed courses (a
simple branded card with name, course and date, shareable as an image), bookmarks.

Data: categories, courses (title, cover, instructor, price, level, description, outcomes),
sections, lessons (title, type, duration, content or video, free), quizzes, questions
(prompt, options, answer, explanation), enrolments, progress, attempts.

Minimums for a first build: 8 courses with generated covers across 4 categories, 3 fully
built courses with 3 sections and 4 to 6 lessons each, 3 quizzes with 8 questions,
working progress, streaks and quiz results.

Signature: A lesson progress ring that fills, flash cards that flip with a spring (FlipCard) and swipe away, and a success check at the end of a lesson.
